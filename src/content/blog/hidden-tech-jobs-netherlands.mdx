## The job boards only show you half the market

LinkedIn and Indeed are useful, but they share a blind spot: they mostly show jobs from companies that **pay to post**. A huge number of roles — especially at smaller Dutch companies — only ever appear on the company's own careers page. No board, no recruiter, no feed. If you're not manually checking dozens of company sites, those jobs are invisible to you.

So we built a crawler that checks them for you. Every day, [CubeA](/jobs) scans **1,000+ Dutch company career pages** and pulls in the roles directly from the source — currently around **13,000 live jobs**, including **~1,100 "hidden gems"**: roles from smaller companies that rarely surface on the big boards at all.

Here's what that data shows about where the hidden work really is.

## Hidden roles cluster in a handful of cities

No surprise that the big tech hubs dominate — but the *concentration* is striking. The clear leaders for open tech roles right now:

- **[Amsterdam](/jobs/amsterdam)** — by far the deepest market.
- **[Utrecht](/jobs/utrecht)** — punching well above its size.
- **[Eindhoven](/jobs/eindhoven)** — the Brainport effect is real, especially in hardware and engineering.
- **[Rotterdam](/jobs/rotterdam)** — smaller, but a steady stream of roles.

If you're open to relocating within the Netherlands, widening your search beyond Amsterdam opens up a meaningful chunk of roles most people never look at.

## Software engineering leads, but it's not the whole story

By role, software and platform engineering make up the largest share of what we crawl — but there's real depth in [data roles](/jobs/data), [product management](/jobs/product-manager), and [DevOps & cloud](/jobs/devops) too. You can browse any of these directly:

- [Software Engineer jobs](/jobs/software-engineer)
- [Developer jobs](/jobs/developer)
- [Data jobs](/jobs/data)
- [Security jobs](/jobs/security)

## How to actually use this

A few tips if you're hunting:

- **Filter for hidden gems.** The whole point of CubeA is the roles you *won't* find elsewhere — start with the [hidden gems](/hidden-gems) view.
- **Search by your city and role together** — e.g. engineering roles in Amsterdam or Eindhoven — instead of scrolling one giant list.
- **Apply directly.** Every listing links straight to the company's own application page. No recruiters, no middlemen, no markup.

The best job for you might be at a company you've never heard of, that you'd never think to check. That's exactly the kind of role we're trying to put in front of you.
